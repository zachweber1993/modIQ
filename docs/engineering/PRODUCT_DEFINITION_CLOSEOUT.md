## Product Mission

modIQ is an evidence-based assessment tool for Farming Simulator mods. This document records the transition from defining the product to designing it.

---

## Purpose

A Product Definition phase occurred and concluded outside the repository. This document records that it happened, what it settled, and what phase follows — nothing more. It is not a specification and does not modify or supersede `Vision.md`, `Principles.md`, `ProductSpecification.md`, `Architecture.md`, or any engineering documentation.

## Product Definition Phase — Closed

An Assessment Experience Program and initial Product Experience work explored the conceptual experience of performing an Assessment in modIQ. That work concluded with no repository, governance, or architectural impact (per its own Repository Integration Review) and remains external product-design material. A subsequent Product Experience Session Handoff was reviewed and accepted, closing further philosophical exploration and adopting a fixed set of product decisions. This document records that closure.

---

## Product Design Phase — Active

The project has entered the Product Design phase. The objective of this phase is to design and specify Version 1 of the modIQ assessment experience using the established architectural and product foundations. Future work should build upon the established product foundations through concrete product decisions. Established foundations should only be revisited when new evidence or product requirements create a genuine forcing function. Current initiative: Workspace Evolution, approached as a practical product-design exercise.

---

## Stable Product Principles

- The Assessment remains the product.
- Information exists to increase understanding.
- Recommendations are supported by Findings; Findings are supported by Evidence.
- modIQ performs work on behalf of the user whenever possible; users never perform work modIQ can perform itself.
- Progressive understanding is the preferred information model.
- Curiosity should never create friction.
- The workspace remains receptive to additional material throughout an Assessment.

---

## Product Design Filter

Every future product proposal should first answer:

- Does this make getting an assessment easier?
- Does this make understanding the assessment easier?
- Does this make acting on the assessment easier?

If the answer to all three is no, the proposal should generally be deferred beyond Version 1.
